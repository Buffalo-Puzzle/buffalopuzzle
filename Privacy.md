Privacy Policy for Buffalo Puzzle
Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Buffalo Puzzle ("the App") is developed and maintained by an independent individual developer ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Buffalo Puzzle handles information when you download, access, or use the App.

1. Information We Collect
1.1 Game Data Stored on Your Device
Buffalo Puzzle is designed to operate without an account or a developer-operated server. Game progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

We do not intentionally collect or transmit your local game progress, scores, or preferences to our own servers. We do not provide cloud synchronization or online game progress storage operated by us.

1.2 Device Information and Usage Data
The App integrates the Meta (Facebook) Software Development Kit (SDK) provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates (collectively, "Meta"). Depending on how the App is configured and which features you use, the Meta SDK may process information when you use the App. This may include, for example:

Device and technical information (such as device model, operating system version, locale or language settings, and similar diagnostics needed for the SDK to operate).
Identifiers (such as device or advertising identifiers where permitted and enabled on your device, and identifiers used for app functionality, security, analytics, or related purposes as implemented in the App).
App interaction and event data (such as launches, sessions, in-app actions, errors or crashes, and similar usage or diagnostic events that may be collected through the SDK).
IP address and related network data processed in the ordinary course of providing online services.
Account or authentication-related information if you choose to use Meta-supported login or social features.
We do not operate our own server that stores your local game progress for the purposes described in Section 1.1. Information collected through the Meta SDK is processed by Meta under Meta's terms and policies, as described in Section 3.

Tracking that requires consent under Apple's rules will be handled in line with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and your device settings, where applicable. Meta may also process information in accordance with Meta's policies and your Meta account or device settings, as applicable.

Your device's operating system or Apple may process certain technical or diagnostic information independently of us. Such processing is governed by Apple's applicable policies and settings.

1.3 Information You Provide Through Customer Support
If you contact us at support@buffalopuzzle.com, we may receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message.

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, provide assistance, and address the issue you report.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your support requests unless it is necessary to resolve your issue.

2. How We Use Information
We use information only as necessary to:

Operate the App and provide its gameplay features.
Store and maintain game progress and preferences locally on your device.
Respond to support requests that you voluntarily submit.
Address technical issues reported by users.
Enable functionality provided through the Meta SDK (such as authentication, analytics, app stability, security, or related purposes as implemented in the App and permitted by your settings).
We do not sell personal information. We do not use local-only game progress stored on your device (as described in Section 1.1) for targeted advertising on our own behalf, because we do not receive that progress on our servers.

Buffalo Puzzle does not currently use Game Center, in-app purchases, or a developer-operated backend server.

If we introduce additional third-party services or materially change the App's data practices, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Security
4.1 Local Game Data
Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device rather than on our servers.

This information generally remains on your device until it is deleted through an available in-game function, removed through device settings, or otherwise removed by the operating system. Depending on your device settings, local data may also be included in device backups.

We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of your local game progress.

4.2 Customer Support Information
If you email us, we retain your correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, resolve the issue, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

4.3 Security
Information processed through the Meta SDK is subject to Meta's retention and security practices described in Meta's policies. We do not operate Meta's systems.

We take reasonable steps to protect information that we receive directly (for example, support emails). However, no method of electronic storage or communication is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices
Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you (where we act as a controller for such information).

You may contact us at support@buffalopuzzle.com to exercise these rights or ask questions about our privacy practices.

Because game progress is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers, we generally cannot access, retrieve, or remotely delete that progress. You can manage locally stored game data through any available in-game controls or your device's settings.

For information processed by Meta, including available choices and rights requests where applicable, please review Meta's privacy resources and your device settings (including Apple's tracking permissions, where applicable).

6. Children's Privacy
Buffalo Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App for our own independent use beyond what may occur through integrated third-party services subject to your choices and applicable platform rules. The App does not provide user accounts or online chat operated by us. If you use Meta-related features, Meta's services may have age restrictions and additional rules under Meta's terms.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through customer support, please contact us at support@buffalopuzzle.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps to delete the information where required by applicable law.

If the App's intended audience or data practices change, we will review and update this section as necessary.

7. International Users
Your privacy rights may vary depending on the country or region in which you reside. Meta and Apple process information globally as described in their respective policies.

Where applicable, we will handle personal information in accordance with relevant privacy and data protection laws. You may contact us using the details below if you have questions about your rights or how we handle information.
