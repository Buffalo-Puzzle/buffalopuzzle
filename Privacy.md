# Privacy Policy for Buffalo Puzzle

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

Buffalo Puzzle ("the App") is developed and maintained by an independent individual developer ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Buffalo Puzzle handles information when you download, access, or use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Game Data Stored on Your Device

Buffalo Puzzle is designed to operate without an account or a developer-operated server. Game progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

We do not intentionally collect or transmit your local game progress, scores, or preferences to our own servers. We do not provide cloud synchronization or online game progress storage.

### 1.2 Device Information and Usage Data

Based on the App's current configuration, we do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs, and we do not intentionally collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, gameplay analytics, or behavioral tracking data.

The App does not use advertising tracking or cross-app tracking.

Your device's operating system or Apple may process certain technical or diagnostic information independently of us. Such processing is governed by Apple's applicable policies and settings.

### 1.3 Information You Provide Through Customer Support

If you contact us at buffalopuzzle@gmail.com, we may receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message.

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, provide assistance, and address the issue you report.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your support requests unless it is necessary to resolve your issue.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information only as necessary to:

- Operate the App and provide its gameplay features.
- Store and maintain game progress and preferences locally on your device.
- Respond to support requests that you voluntarily submit.
- Address technical issues reported by users.

We do not sell personal information, use personal information for targeted advertising, or use gameplay data for advertising analytics.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Buffalo Puzzle does not currently integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not currently use Game Center, in-app purchases, or a developer-operated backend server.

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may process information in connection with downloading, installing, updating, or using iOS and its platform services. Apple's processing of such information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

We do not receive or control information that Apple processes independently unless Apple makes that information available to us.

If we introduce third-party services or change the App's data practices in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

## 4. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

### 4.1 Local Game Data

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device rather than on our servers.

This information generally remains on your device until it is deleted through an available in-game function, removed through device settings, or otherwise removed by the operating system. Depending on your device settings, local data may also be included in device backups.

We do not maintain a separate server-side copy of your local game progress.

### 4.2 Customer Support Information

If you email us, we retain your correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, resolve the issue, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

### 4.3 Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that we receive directly. However, no method of electronic storage or communication is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you.

You may contact us at buffalopuzzle@gmail.com to exercise these rights or ask questions about our privacy practices.

Because game progress is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers, we generally cannot access, retrieve, or remotely delete that progress. You can manage locally stored game data through any available in-game controls or your device's settings.

The App does not use advertising tracking, so it does not provide an in-app advertising tracking consent mechanism.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Buffalo Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. The App does not provide user accounts, online chat, or a mechanism for users to submit personal information directly within the game.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through customer support, please contact us at buffalopuzzle@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps to delete the information where required by applicable law.

If the App's intended audience or data practices change, we will review and update this section as necessary.

## 7. International Users

Your privacy rights may vary depending on the country or region in which you reside.

Where applicable, we will handle personal information in accordance with relevant privacy and data protection laws. You may contact us using the details below if you have questions about your rights or how we handle information.
